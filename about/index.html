About Surendra

Telling the story, then helping tell it straight.

Journalist, press coordinator and student of society: a profile of the person behind the press desk.

Surendra Bajagain is a media and communications professional whose work spans journalism, public communication and press coordination for elected leaders.

He began his career in Nepal's newsrooms, reporting for national outlets including Nepal Live, Nepalwatch, Ukeraa, Baahrakhari, Ekagaj and Routine of Nepal Bandh. Much of that work was investigative: stories built on documents, field visits and patient verification rather than press releases.

That newsroom grounding shaped the next chapter. At Kathmandu Metropolitan City he served as press coordinator in the Mayor's Secretariat under Balendra Shah, managing media relations and keeping the city's communication open and timely.

In March 2026, after Balendra Shah became Prime Minister, Surendra was appointed Press Facilitator to the Prime Minister. In that role he connects the head of government with journalists across the country and beyond.

Alongside his work he is completing a master's degree in Sociology at Tribhuvan University, an interest that keeps his attention on how public decisions land in ordinary lives.

Principles

How he works

On his appointment, Surendra pledged to carry out the responsibility with dedication, combining experience with continued study and placing the nation first. These principles follow from that.

01

Accuracy

Verify before you publish, whether in a newsroom or a press office.

02

Openness

Journalists should be able to reach the people they need to question.

03

Public first

Communication is a public service, measured by what citizens understand.

04

Rootedness

Keep close to home: to Lalitpur, and to the realities outside the capital.
