Surendra Bajagain is a seasoned journalist and public communicator based in Kathmandu, Nepal[cite: 7]. Operating at the direct intersection of government institutions (including OPMCM and Kathmandu Metropolitan City Office), mainstream media channels, and civic outreach, he specializes in translating complex policy work into transparent public narratives.

With extensive experience across municipal masterplan surveys, executive press desks, and nationwide digital literacy initiatives, Surendra brings strategic oversight to governance communications and media strategy.
