Short bio

Surendra Bajagain is a Nepali journalist and communications professional who serves as Press Facilitator to the Prime Minister of Nepal. He previously led press coordination for the Mayor of Kathmandu Metropolitan City.

Full bio

Surendra Bajagain is a Nepali journalist and media and communications professional. Since March 2026 he has served as Press Facilitator to Prime Minister Balendra Shah, coordinating the Prime Minister's engagement with the media. He previously served as press coordinator in the Mayor's Secretariat at Kathmandu Metropolitan City. Before entering public service, he reported for national outlets including Nepal Live, Nepalwatch, Ukeraa, Baahrakhari and Ekagaj, with a focus on investigative journalism. He holds a bachelor's degree in Rural Development and is pursuing a master's in Sociology at Tribhuvan University. He is from Mahankal Rural Municipality in Lalitpur.
